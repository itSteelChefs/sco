# Stage 003 Batch 8 — Owner Decisions

Batch 8 is authorized for RFQ, supplier quote, negotiation and approved buy-side cost only. Unqualified sources are prohibited; there is no eligibility override. Creator and approver are separate by default, with explicit `purchase_cost.self_approve` for Owner/Super Admin only and complete audit evidence. Purchase cost snapshots are immutable and RFQ-item scoped. Alternative product proposals require explicit SCO review. Quote documents are optional and private. Closed RFQs cannot reopen; repeat sourcing creates a new RFQ. Quote validity is optional, expired revisions cannot create new approvals without reconfirmation. Split awards are supported with database-backed quantity protection. Original currencies and tax/payment/delivery terms are preserved without FX automation. No customer pricing, margin, Partner Offer/PO, funding, portal, OCR, AI, ERP, or production work is included.

When an Alternative Product HTTP endpoint is introduced, its acceptance criteria must prove that actor identity comes exclusively from the authenticated `sco_session` / `request.scoUser` and cannot be overridden through a request body, header, query parameter, or client-supplied UUID.
